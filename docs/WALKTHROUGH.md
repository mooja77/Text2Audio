# Text2Audio short walkthrough

Watch the [captioned current-interface walkthrough](../web/help/text2audio-walkthrough.mp4), or follow the matching steps:

1. On **Create**, add `.txt`/Markdown or choose **Use safe example**.
2. Check the detected chapter names and order.
3. Pick a narrator and use **Preview**.
4. Select **Generate Audiobook**. If the browser refreshes, it reconnects to the active job while the app stays running.
5. Open **Library** and listen to the finished book.

First value is a successfully saved `.m4b` plus its local manifest. Opening Studio, choosing a file, or starting a job does not count.
